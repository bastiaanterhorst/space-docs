title: Search
description: Find any task, wherever it lives in Sonto
---

Search finds your tasks no matter where they are — in a project, an area, the inbox, a past day, or a
future week. Results can be sorted and filtered so you can zero in quickly.

## Search on Mac

Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>\\</kbd> to open search. The same panel lets you jump to any screen in the
app, so you can navigate and search from one place. Start typing to match tasks by their text.

## Search on iPhone

Tap the **magnifying glass** on your Home Screen to search or navigate from anywhere in the app.

The search field sits at the bottom of the screen, between a **filter** button for sorting and
filtering and an **×** that clears your search. When your results carry [tags](/reference/tags),
their tags float just above the field, so you can narrow the results with a tap while you type.

## Sorting and filtering

Within results you can sort and filter to narrow things down — handy when a common word matches many
tasks. Combine search with [tags](/reference/tags) to focus on a specific slice of your work. Turn on
`Only Tasks with Attachments` to see only tasks that have [files attached](/reference/attachments).

## Meeting notes

With **Search in Notes** turned on, search also finds your
[private event notes](/reference/private-event-notes), by their text or the title of their event. They appear in
a **Meeting Notes** section below the matching tasks. Choose one to open it in focus, even when its event
is no longer on your calendar.

## Availability

Search is available on both **Mac** and **iPhone**.
