Sonto is built to be used however you need to use it — by voice, by keyboard, by switch, or at whatever
text size suits your eyes. This page covers what's supported and where to turn it on.

Most of it comes from your device's own accessibility settings, which Sonto follows. The one setting
that lives inside Sonto is **Text size**.

## Text size

Sonto can render everything smaller or larger, independently of your system text size.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mac">
    Open `Settings` → `General` and drag the **Text size** slider. There are five steps: **Small**,
    **Standard**, **Large**, **Extra Large** and **Huge**. Standard is the default.

    The whole interface scales with it, not just the text — rows, spacing and the sidebar grow
    together, so nothing crowds.

    You can also change it without opening Settings, from the **View** menu: **Zoom In**
    (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>+</kbd>), **Zoom Out** (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>&minus;</kbd>) and
    **Actual Size** (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>0</kbd>) to go back to Standard.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPhone & iPad">
    Open `Settings` → `General`. **Use System Text Size** is on by default, so Sonto follows the Dynamic
    Type size you've set for the whole device.

    Turn it off to pin Sonto to a size of its own, then pick one with the slider — **Small**,
    **Standard**, **Large**, **Extra Large** or **Huge**. The step marked **Standard** is your system
    default, so you can always find your way back.

    Leave the toggle **on** if you use one of the accessibility text sizes: those are larger than the
    in-app slider reaches, and Sonto follows them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  Settings and the Backup & Import window deliberately stay at your system text size. Both are fixed-size
  windows, so they'd have nowhere to grow.
</Info>

## VoiceOver

Sonto works with VoiceOver on both Mac and iPhone.

A task reads as a single item — its name, state, schedule and tags in one pass, rather than as a
scattering of separate controls. From there, VoiceOver's actions give you everything the `…` menu
gives a sighted user:

- Complete the task, or toggle it in progress.
- Quick-schedule it.
- **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Move to Top**, **Move to Bottom** — reordering without dragging.
- Delete it.

Lists announce themselves by name, so you know whether you're in `Overdue`, a named group, or the main
task list. Headings are marked up as headers, so you can jump between them with the rotor. Calendar
events read with their details and can be opened or acted on directly.

Sonto also speaks up when something happens out of view: when a completed task can still be undone,
when a bulk move, schedule or delete finishes and how many tasks it touched, and when a planning session
moves to its next step.

<Info>
  These announcements are separate from Sonto's sound effects — you'll hear them even with sounds
  turned off in `Settings` → `General`.
</Info>

## Switch Control and Voice Control

Sonto's core screens name and label their controls, which is what Switch Control and Voice Control need
to reach them. That includes the ones that are easy to miss: the checkbox, the weekday picker in a
repeating task, the panel controls on Mac, and the action bar on iPhone.

## System settings Sonto respects

Turn these on in your device's accessibility settings and Sonto follows them:

| Setting | What changes in Sonto |
| --- | --- |
| **Reduce Motion** | Rows open without animating, drag springs become near-instant, and pulses and swipe bounces stop. |
| **Reduce Transparency** | Translucent surfaces become opaque. |
| **Differentiate Without Colour** | Sonto adds a shape as well as a colour — a glyph on urgent deadlines, on priority checkboxes, and on overdue text — so nothing depends on colour alone. |
| **Larger Text / Dynamic Type** | Sonto scales with it, unless you've pinned your own size above. |
| **Large Content Viewer** (iPhone) | Press and hold a control in the action bar to see its name enlarged. |

## Using Sonto from the keyboard

On Mac you can run Sonto entirely from the keyboard — navigating, editing, scheduling, and reordering.
See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/other-topics/keyboard-shortcuts) for the full list.

## Availability

Accessibility support covers both **Mac** and **iPhone & iPad**. Text size is set inside Sonto on both.
