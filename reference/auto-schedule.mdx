---
title: Auto-schedule
description: Let Sonto find an opening on your calendar for a task
---

Auto-scheduling places a task on your [calendar](/reference/calendar) for you, finding a free slot that
respects your working hours and the commitments you already have.

## Find time on a calendar

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mac">
    Select a task and press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd>, or right-click it and choose **Find Time on
    Calendar**. It's also in the **Tasks** menu and in the action bar's `…` menu.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPhone & iPad">
    Open the task and choose **Find Time on Calendar** from the `…` menu at the bottom of the editor. With
    tasks selected, it's in the action bar's `…` menu. On iPad, it's also in the **Tasks** menu, with
    <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> on a keyboard.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Sonto looks at your calendar, skips over existing events, stays inside your working hours, and creates a
time block in the first opening that fits. It starts the block on the quarter hour — 10:15 rather than
10:07 — whenever the block still fits there. The event is linked to the task, just like dragging a task
onto the calendar yourself.

  <img src="/images/auto-schedule/find-opening.png" alt="A task being auto-scheduled into a free slot on the calendar" />
</Frame>

### Which day

- **A task scheduled for today** — Sonto looks for an opening between now and the end of your working
  hours.
- **A task scheduled for a later day** — Sonto looks on that day.
- **An unscheduled task, a task planned for a week, or an overdue task** — Sonto schedules it for today
  first, then finds time.

A task that already has a time block can't be auto-scheduled again; move its event instead.

### How long

The block uses your **default event duration** — 30 minutes unless you change it. To book half or double
that, pick the length from the **Find Time on Calendar** menu, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> +
<kbd>F</kbd> for half and <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> for double.

### When nothing fits

If no opening is long enough within your working hours, Sonto tells you — **No Free Time Found** — rather
than booking outside them or on another day. A task it moved to today stays scheduled for today, just
without a time block.

## Working hours

Auto-scheduling only places tasks within your **working hours**, so it never books your evenings unless
you tell it to. Set your working hours and default event duration in **Settings → Scheduling**. See
[Settings](/reference/settings#scheduling).

## Used by repeating tasks

[Repeating tasks](/reference/repeating-tasks) can auto-schedule each occurrence, finding a fresh opening
every time instead of using a fixed time.

## Availability

Auto-scheduling works on **Mac**, **iPhone** and **iPad**. The keyboard shortcuts need a Mac, or an iPad
with a keyboard.
