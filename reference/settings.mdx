title: Settings
description: What you can configure in Sonto, and where to find it

This page is a map of what you can adjust in Sonto. Settings are specific to each device — they don't
sync, so a change you make on your Mac won't follow you to your iPhone.

## Where it lives

- **Mac** — open the `Sonto` menu and choose `Settings…`, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>,</kbd>.
  Settings open in their own window, with a sidebar listing the panes below and back and forward
  buttons for the pages that nest inside them.
- **iPhone & iPad** — tap the settings entry, and pick a section from the list. The sections are the
  same, minus **AI**, and **About** is called **Help**.

## General

Sonto's overall look and feel.

- **Appearance** — light, dark, or follow your system setting.
- **Text size** — make everything in Sonto smaller or larger, independently of your system setting.
  See [Text size](/reference/accessibility#text-size).
- **App icon** — choose an alternative icon for your dock or Home Screen.
- **Sidebar badges** — whether the sidebar counts your `Inbox`, `Today`, both, or neither.
- **Sound effects** — the small sounds Sonto plays when you complete a task.
- **Sync status** — whether iCloud sync is healthy, in progress, or stalled. See
  [Sync](/reference/sync). On iPhone this sits at the top of the settings list rather than inside
  General.

Mac adds one more: **Dock icon when Sonto is running** — always, only in dark mode, or never.

## Tasks

How tasks behave as you work through them.

- **Enable natural language dates** — whether Sonto reads dates, times, projects and tags out of a
  task's name as you type. See [Natural language input](/reference/natural-language-input).
- **Move overdue tasks to…** — where a task goes when its day passes: `Today`, `This week`, or nowhere.
  See [Overdue tasks](/reference/overdue-tasks).
- **Automatically clear completed…** — after 30, 60 or 90 days, or never. You can set this separately
  for tasks in projects, tasks in areas, and other tasks.
- **Global hotkeys** (Mac) — two system-wide hotkeys for adding a task to your inbox or to today, even
  when Sonto is in the background. See [Quick capture](/reference/quick-capture).

## Planning

The rhythm of your planning sessions. See [Notifications & Reminders](/reference/notifications).

- **Daily planning reminders** — when Sonto nudges you to plan, and on which days.
- **Also plan weekend days** — whether Saturday and Sunday take part in daily planning.
- **Quarterly planning reminder** — when to prompt you for the next quarter or year.
- **Offer to continue planning** — whether Sonto suggests carrying on to the next step.
- **Copy tasks when dragging in planning** — drag a task into a plan to copy it rather than move it.

## Calendars

Connecting Sonto to the calendars on your device. See [Calendar](/reference/calendar).

- **Calendar access** — grant Sonto permission to read your calendars, and choose which ones to show.
- **Accounts** — connect a Google account, or add others in your system settings.
- **Default calendar** — where Sonto puts the events it creates.
- **Hide task names on calendar events** — keep task names private on a shared calendar.

## Scheduling

How Sonto places work on your calendar.

- **Working hours** — the hours Sonto treats as available when it
  [auto-schedules](/reference/auto-schedule) a task, including for repeating tasks.
- **Default event duration** — the length of a time block Sonto creates.
- **First day of the week** — which day your week screen starts on.
- **Calendar density** — how tightly the calendar packs its hours.

## Tags

Create, rename and delete your [tags](/reference/tags). Tags you remove here disappear from every task,
project and area that carried them.

## AI

- **AI Access** — the main switch for the MCP server. When off, no AI tool can reach Sonto, and the
  `sonto` CLI stops working too.
- **Allow changes** — off by default. Turn it on to let an AI tool create, edit and complete items.
- **Install Claude Desktop Connector** — one-click setup for Claude Desktop.
- **Use the CLI or connect to other AI Agents** — opens an **Advanced** page holding the `Endpoint`,
  `Port`, pairing code, connected clients, and the `CLI Path` with a **Copy** button.

See [Connect to AI Agents](/reference/ai-with-mcp) and the [Sonto CLI](/reference/sonto-cli).

## About

Your version and build number, open-source licences, and the terms and privacy policy. On iPhone this
section is called **Help**.

<Info>
  Backups, restore, import and delete live in the **Backup & Import** window rather than Settings. See
  [Backup & Import](/reference/manage-data).
</Info>

## Availability

Settings exist on both **Mac** and **iPhone & iPad**. Two things are **Mac-only**: **global hotkeys**,
and the whole **AI** pane — the MCP server and the CLI run on the Mac. **Backup & Import** is on every
platform; on Mac it lives outside Settings. Importing from **Things** is Mac-only, because Things for
iOS has no export and its database is not readable by other apps.
