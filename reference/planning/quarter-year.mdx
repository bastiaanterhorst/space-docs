Yearly and quarterly **plans** are documents you create to capture your [intention](/reference/glossary#intention),
your [groups](/reference/glossary#group), and the goals or [milestones](/reference/glossary#milestone)
under them. Unlike planning a day or week — which simply schedules tasks — a plan is a thing in its own
right that lives in your sidebar.

## Starting a plan

There are a few ways to begin:

- On Mac, click `File → Planning → Plan your Quarter or Year…`.
- Hover the **+** at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **New Plan**.
- Sonto also prompts you near the start of a new quarter or year.
- On iPhone and iPad, tap the **+** and choose **New Plan**.

  <img src="/images/planning/plans-new.png" alt="Creating a new plan from the sidebar" />
</Frame>

## The guided flow

Creating a plan puts you in planning mode, which walks you through four steps — **Review → Intention →
Plan → Finalize** — and then you commit. For the full step-by-step, see the
[tutorial](/guides/planning-your-year-and-quarter).


It's recommended to plan your **year before your quarter**, and your **quarter before your week or
day** — each level anchors the next. That said, starting at the quarter is perfectly fine if a year
feels like too much.

## Plans live in your sidebar

A plan is a document — think of it as a roadmap with your goals and vision — and it lives in your
sidebar, always a click away.

<Frame caption="Yearly and quarterly plans live in the sidebar">
  <img src="/images/planning/plans-sidebar.png" alt="Yearly and quarterly plans listed in the Sonto sidebar" />
</Frame>

## Updating plan progress

As the quarter or year unfolds, click a plan in the sidebar and check off what you've completed; long-
click a checkbox to mark something in progress. Plans behave much like projects.

When a plan is finished — at the end of the period, or once you've done what you set out to — **close**
it by clicking the green progress pie next to its title. Closed plans leave the sidebar to keep it
uncluttered; you can always find them in the Plans area.

<Frame caption="A closed plan">
  <img src="/images/planning/plan-closed.png" alt="A closed plan shown with a completed progress indicator" />
</Frame>

## Plan states

A small badge below a plan's title shows its state:

- **Active** — planned, current, and open. The stuff to focus on.
- **Past** — planned but no longer current, and still open. Worth reviewing and closing.
- **Future** — planned but starts later. Prepared, but not yet begun.
- **Draft** — you haven't finished planning it yet.
- **Closed** — reviewed and done.

## Linking plans

When you create a plan, Sonto links it to relevant others — the quarter or year before it, or the year
a quarter belongs to. Linking lets Sonto show the right previous plan during the Review step, and lets
you jump quickly between plans. Set links in the new-plan dialog, and change them later on the plan page.

## The Plans area

New plans are gathered in a `🎯 Plans` area, created automatically with your first plan. Closed plans
leave the sidebar but remain here, so current and past plans are always in one place.

<Frame caption="All your plans gathered in one place">
  <img src="/images/planning/plans-area.png" alt="The Plans area gathering all current and past plans in one place" />
</Frame>

## Multiple plans for the same period

You can create more than one plan for the same period — for example, separate work and personal plans,
so you can share your work plan without revealing everything personal. Double-click a plan's title to
rename it to something more descriptive.
