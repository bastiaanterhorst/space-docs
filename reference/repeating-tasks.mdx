A repeating task comes back automatically, so you only set it up once. Sonto supports two repeat styles
and can optionally add each occurrence to your calendar.

## Make a task repeat

Open a task and choose **Edit Repetition** — on Mac you can also right-click the task and pick it from
the menu. From there you set how the task should recur.

<Frame caption="Editing a task's repetition">
  <img src="/images/tasks/recurring.png" alt="The Edit Repetition dialog for setting how a task repeats" />
</Frame>

## Two ways to repeat

- **Regularly** — the task reappears on set dates (for example, every Monday, or the first of the
  month), regardless of whether you finished the previous one.
- **When Completed** — the next occurrence is created only once you complete the current one. This suits
  tasks where the interval matters from when you *actually* did it, like "water the plants every 5 days".

## Monthly: choose the day

When you repeat a task **Regularly** and set **Repeat** to **Monthly**, an **On** row appears below it.
It decides where in each month the task lands. You can pick one of two kinds of day:

- **The day of the month** — for example, **the 16th**.
- **The weekday of the month** — for example, **the third Friday**.

Sonto offers these options based on the day the task is scheduled for. A task on Friday 16 October offers
**the 16th** and **the third Friday**. If the task isn't scheduled on a day (it's on a week, or not
scheduled at all), Sonto uses today. If you reschedule the task while the editor is open, the options follow
the new day and keep the kind you chose.

### Days that not every month has

Some days don't occur in every month. Sonto handles them like this:

| You choose | In a month without that day |
|---|---|
| **the 29th** or **the 30th** | The task lands on the last day of the month, such as 28 February. |
| **the last day** (offered for the 31st) | The task always lands on the month's last day. |
| **the last Friday** (offered for a fifth weekday) | The task lands on the last Friday, which is the fifth or the fourth Friday. |

When the task's day is the last of its kind, Sonto also offers a **last** option next to the regular one.
For example, 30 April offers **the 30th** and **the last day**. A Friday that is the fourth and final Friday
of its month offers **the fourth Friday** and **the last Friday**.

The **On** row appears only for **Monthly**. It's hidden for **When Completed** and for the other
schedules, including **Custom**.

<Info>
  A monthly or yearly repetition that starts on a day some months lack stays on that day. A series that
  starts on 31 January lands on the last day of February and is back on the 31st in March. A yearly
  repetition from 29 February lands on 28 February in other years.
</Info>

## Change an existing repetition

Open **Edit Repetition** on any task in the series. Changes that keep the pattern, such as the time or
length of its calendar events, are saved with **Update Repetition**. When you change the pattern itself, such as the **On**
day, the button changes to **Re-create Repetition**. Sonto then replaces the open future tasks in the series
with new ones that follow the new pattern, counting from the day of the task you opened the editor on. That
task stays where it is. Completed tasks aren't touched.

Monthly repetitions you set up before the **On** row existed keep repeating on their day of the month.
The row shows that day, so nothing changes until you pick something else.


## Add repeating tasks to your calendar

A repeating task can also create a calendar event for each occurrence. You can have Sonto place the
event at a **fixed time**, or let it **find an opening** in your schedule that respects your working
hours and existing commitments — see [Auto-schedule](/reference/auto-schedule).

## Availability

Repeating tasks can be created and completed on **Mac**, **iPhone** and **iPad**. Finding an opening for
a calendar event uses [auto-scheduling](/reference/auto-schedule).
