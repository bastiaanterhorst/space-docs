## No Sonto account

Sonto doesn't have its own accounts. You don't create a login, and you don't hand over an email address.
Your data lives in your own [iCloud](/reference/sync) and syncs through it, so the only "account"
involved is your Apple Account. See [Privacy & Data](/reference/privacy-and-data).

## Restoring a purchase

If you buy Sonto and later sign in on another device — or reinstall it — use **Restore** on the screen asking you upgrade to
bring the purchase across. Because purchases are tied to your Apple Account, there's nothing else to
recover. Your data will sync automatically (make sure you're logged in on the same Apple Account).
