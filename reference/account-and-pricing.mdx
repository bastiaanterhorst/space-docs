## No Sonto account

Sonto doesn't have its own accounts. You don't create a login, and you don't hand over an email address.
Your data lives in your own [iCloud](/reference/sync) and syncs through it, so the only "account"
involved is your Apple Account. See [Privacy & Data](/reference/privacy-and-data).

## Free, and Sonto Pro

Planning in Sonto is free: tasks, projects, areas, your year, quarter, week and day, notes, backup and
export. That part stays free, and nothing you have already made is ever taken away.

**Sonto Pro** adds powerful features around time blocking, organisation and automation:

- Your calendars in Sonto, and time blocked for tasks on your calendar
- Repeating tasks
- Tags across projects and areas
- [AI access](/reference/ai-with-mcp) on the Mac, including the [Sonto CLI](/reference/sonto-cli)
- The custom app icons

Pro is free to try for a week, and then available as a monthly, yearly or one-time payment. Without
Pro, Sonto shows one reminder a day after your first fortnight — that is the only nudge.

Sonto is made by one independent developer, with no ads, no investors and no tracking. Paying for Pro
is also what keeps that possible.

## Restoring a purchase

If you upgrade and later sign in on another device — or reinstall Sonto — use **Restore Purchases** to
bring your upgrade across. Because purchases are tied to your Apple Account, there's nothing else to
recover.

## Availability

Sonto is available on **Mac**, **iPhone** and **iPad** from the App Store, and needs macOS 26, iOS 26
or iPadOS 26 or later — see [System requirements](/reference/system-requirements). A purchase applies
across your devices on the same Apple Account.
