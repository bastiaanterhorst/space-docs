---
title: Overdue Tasks
description: How Space surfaces tasks you scheduled but didn't finish
---

When a task was scheduled for a day that has passed and you didn't complete it, Space presents it
clearly as **overdue** rather than letting it quietly slip out of view. You then decide what to do with
it, one task at a time.

## Keep or postpone

Each overdue task comes with two quick actions:

- **Keep** — bring the task into today, so you tackle it now.
- **Postpone** — push it to a later day, when you'll realistically get to it.

This keeps your day honest: nothing disappears, and you make a deliberate choice instead of carrying an
ever-growing backlog.
