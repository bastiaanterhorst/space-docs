Sonto's widgets keep your tasks in view without opening the app. Tick tasks off right from the widget, or
tap one to jump to it in Sonto.

## Add a widget

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone & iPad">
    - **Home Screen** — touch and hold an empty area, tap the **+** in the corner, search for **Sonto**,
      pick a size, and add it.
    - **Lock Screen** — touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customise**, choose the widget area, and add
      a **Sonto** widget.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mac">
    Add Sonto widgets the same way you add any widget: click the date/time in the menu bar to open
    Notification Centre, scroll down, click **Edit Widgets**, and choose **Sonto**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The Lists widget

The **Lists** widget shows the tasks still to do in one list, with the number left in its header.

### Choose a list and the plus button

Touch and hold the widget and tap **Edit Widget** — on Mac, right-click it — to change two settings:

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **List** | **Inbox**, **Today** (the default), **Tomorrow**, **This Week** or **Next Week** |
| **Plus Button** | **Current List** (the default) adds the new task to the list the widget shows. **Inbox** and **Today** always add there. **Disabled** removes the button. |

| List | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Inbox** | The tasks in your Inbox |
| **Today** | Today's tasks, with time-blocked tasks first. Overdue tasks show here too, unless **Move overdue tasks to…** is set to `This week` in [Settings](/reference/settings#tasks). |
| **Tomorrow** | Tomorrow's tasks, with time-blocked tasks first |
| **This Week** | Tasks planned for the week as a whole — not tasks on a particular day — plus overdue ones |
| **Next Week** | Tasks planned for next week as a whole |

### Using the widget

- **Tick a checkbox** to complete a task without opening Sonto.
- **Tap a task** to open Sonto with that task highlighted.
- **Tap the header** to open the list in Sonto.
- **Tap +** to open Sonto with a new task ready to type, in the list you chose under **Plus Button**. A
  task added to **This Week** or **Next Week** is planned for the week, not a particular day.

The small widget has no **+** button, and tapping a task there opens the list rather than the task.

## Lock Screen and Control Centre

On iPhone and iPad, two more widgets fit the Lock Screen:

- **Today's progress** — a gauge of how much of today you've completed. Tap it to open Today.
- **Add task** — opens Sonto with a new task in your Inbox, ready to type. It's also a control you can add
  to Control Centre.

## Availability

The **Lists** widget is available on **iPhone**, **iPad** and **Mac**. The Lock Screen widgets and the
Control Centre control are on **iPhone** and **iPad** only.
