Sonto offers widgets so you can see what matters today without opening the app.

## On iPhone

Add a Sonto widget to your **Home Screen** or **Lock Screen** to keep today's tasks in view.

- **Home Screen** — touch and hold an empty area, tap the **+** in the corner, search for **Sonto**,
  pick a size, and add it.
- **Lock Screen** — touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customise**, choose the widget area, and add
  the **Sonto** widget.

## On Mac

Sonto widgets are also available on Mac. Add them from Notification Centre the same way you add any
widget: click the date/time in the menu bar to open Notification Centre, scroll down, click **Edit
Widgets**, and choose **Sonto**.

## Availability

Widgets are available on both **iPhone** (Home Screen and Lock Screen) and **Mac** (Notification
Centre).
