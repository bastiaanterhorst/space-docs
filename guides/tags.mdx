Tags are a flexible, additional way to organise tasks, projects, and areas. Where a task lives in one
project or area, a tag cuts **across** everything — dates, weeks, projects, and areas — so you can bring
related work together and filter any view down to what you want to focus on. Watch below, or read on.

## Create your tags

First, open **Settings** and add a few tags. There's no fixed set — you decide what they mean. A couple
of schemes that work well:

- **By location** — a `home` tag and an `office` tag, for work you can only do in a certain place.
- **By status** — a `waiting` tag, for anything that needs input from someone else before you continue.

## Tag projects, areas, and tasks

You can tag a whole project or area, or an individual task:

- **A project or area** — tag a project `office` if you'll work on it at the office, and everything in
  it travels with that tag.
- **A single task** — in a *Kitchen renovation* project, you might tag just *Order cabinet samples* with
  `office`, because that's the one thing you'll do from your desk.

## Filter any view

Your Day, your Week, and the inside of a project each have a **filter bar**. Pick a tag and the view
narrows to just those items — so you can see, say, only the things to do at the office today.

<Info>
  A tag doesn't schedule a task. For a tagged task to appear on your Day, you still need to schedule it
  for that day — then filter the Day by the tag.
</Info>

You can filter inside a project too, even one that already carries its own tag. Add `waiting` to the
tasks that need input, then filter by `waiting` to see everything that's blocked, in one place.

## Combine tags

A single item can carry more than one tag. *Order cabinet samples* might be tagged both `office` (where
you'll do it) and `waiting` (because you need more information first) — describing the work along two
dimensions at once.

## Stay in the flow

Two things help you focus:

- **Sticky filters** — an active filter is remembered for a couple of hours, so you can settle into one
  tag without reapplying it.
- **Per-view filters** — each screen remembers its own filter. Filter your Week by `waiting` while your
  Day shows `office`; the two don't affect each other.
