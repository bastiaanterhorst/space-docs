title: "Space is now Sonto"
---

**Space is now Sonto.** It's the same app, with the same method, the same features, and all your data
exactly where you left it. Only the name has changed — there's nothing you need to do.

## Why the new name

"Space" turned out to be an impossible name in the App Store. It was hard to find — search for
"space" and you'd wade through hundreds of unrelated apps — and there were simply too many other apps
using the word in their name for us to stand out. A planning app you can't find isn't much use to
anyone.

"Sonto" is short, distinctive, and easy to search for. Type it into the App Store and you'll land on
us, every time.

## What changes for you

Nothing, really:

- **Your data is untouched** — tasks, plans, projects, calendars, and settings all carry over.
- **No new download or sign-in** — the app updates in place.
- **Same method, same workflow** — only the name on the icon is different.

If anything looks off after the update, get in touch at
[support@sonto.app](mailto:support@sonto.app).
