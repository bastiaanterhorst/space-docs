## Updates

### 2.0 — A bold redesign

<Badge color="gray">September 7, 2026</Badge> Sonto 2.0 is cleaner, more legible, and a lot more capable.

NEW
- a cleaner and more legible design
- [make the interface smaller or larger](/reference/accessibility#text-size) on Mac and iOS
- [full accessibility support](/reference/accessibility), including VoiceOver
- [fully supports Chinese](/reference/languages)
- [undo and redo](/reference/tasks#undo-and-redo) almost anything
- [copy, cut and paste tasks](/reference/tasks#copying-and-pasting-tasks) — now on iPhone and iPad too
- [convert a group or task to a project](/reference/projects-areas#convert-a-group-or-task-into-a-project)
- [drag out the +](/reference/tasks#drop-a-task-exactly-where-you-want-it) and drop it precisely where you want to create a new task
- [move tasks up and down](/reference/tasks#reordering-tasks) with <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>&uarr;</kbd> / <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>&darr;</kbd>

IMPROVED

### 1.7.x — Space is now Sonto
<Tip>Same app, new name: Space is now Sonto ([why](/why-sonto)).</Tip>

- Sonto now bundles the [Sonto CLI](/reference/sonto-cli) for your scripting or agentic needs

IMPROVED
- A brand new zero-setup Claude Desktop Connector 
- Repeating tasks are now more robust and predictable
- Many small bug fixes and performance improvements all across the app
- Settings overhaul on the Mac

### 1.4.3 - New notes editor: checklists and formatting + Dutch localization

<Badge color="gray">Mar 20, 2026</Badge> You can now add checklists (sub tasks) in the notes field! Just press the checklist button when editing a task, or type `[]` in the notes field to insert a checklist item. 
You can also indent them to create nested lists. On top of that there is now basic formatting: make text bold or italic. Of course all of this can be controlled with your <a href="/reference/other-topics/keyboard-shortcuts#when-editing-notes">keyboard</a>.

And for all the Dutchies: Je kunt Space nu in het Nederlands gebruiken.

### 1.4 - Brand new Calendar & AI integration

<Badge color="gray">Mar 13, 2026</Badge> A huge release! Space now uses your Mac / iOS native calendars, which means practically ALL calendars are supported. iCloud, Yahoo!, Exchange, Google, plain CalDAV, you name it! This was a very often requested feature and I'm excited to finally ship it. 

Another exciting feature in this release is support for MCP (Model Context Protocol). This is a standard to let application connect to AI tools. So now you can wire up your Claude or ChatGPT to Space and do _just about everything you can do in Space itself, but through AI_. This creates really cool opportunities for long term planning, for instance by pulling in data from your journal, project planning tools, or basically anything else that holds context that can help with your plan. I'd love to hear how you're using MCP support, so I can make it better!

And there is more: several widgets (normal and lock screen), a share extension and app shortcuts deepen Space' integration into the Apple ecosystem.

Finally, a long list of polish, bug fixes and performance improvements.

### 1.3 - Search

<Badge color="gray">Dec 3, 2025</Badge> Quickly find your tasks, no matter where they are hiding! One of the most requested features, Search lets you instantly find your tasks from anywhere. Sorting and filtering included.

Also, a whole bunch of small tweaks and improvements around performance, Mac icons, Siri/Shortcuts, and more.

### 1.2 - Repeating tasks

<Badge color="gray">Nov 7, 2025</Badge> Much requested and finally here: repeating tasks. Tasks can repeat upon completion or on a fixed schedule. You can also have repeating tasks automatically create events on your calendar, either at a fixed time, or by letting Space find time an opening in your schedule.

Finding an opening in your schedule respects your working hours, configurable in Settings. This feature also lets you create events for any task at the click of a button (or by pressing <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd>). Space will respect your working hours and existing commitments and try to find an opening for your task.
